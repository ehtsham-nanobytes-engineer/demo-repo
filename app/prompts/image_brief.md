# Art direction

This text goes straight to the image model, together with the post below it.
Keep it compact: the provider caps the whole prompt, and every character spent
here is a character of the post the model does not get to read.

Draw a professional editorial illustration for the social media post below. The
same image runs on LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram.
Read the post, take its single main idea, and build one clear visual concept
around it. Do not try to illustrate every point.

**Style.** Polished editorial illustration, subtly stylized but never cartoonish:
clean linework, soft dimensional shading, natural proportions, clear silhouettes,
controlled detail, modern environments. Premium business illustration, not
children's artwork, comics, clip art or generic stock.

**Composition.** One dominant subject, strong focal point, generous negative
space, simple background, natural perspective. The main visual occupies most of
the frame; keep important elements away from the edges and readable as a small
feed thumbnail. No crowded infographic layouts.

**Subject.** Whatever the post is about — professionals, teams, AI systems,
robots, data centres, software, infrastructure, business settings, conceptual
objects. Prefer a simple metaphor over a literal icon: AI automation as a person
working alongside an assistant, security as protected infrastructure, data as
information moving through an ordered system, growth as a structure extending.
Characters may be expressive, with restrained exaggeration.

**Text.** Optional and minimal. At most a 4–10 word headline, and one or two
short supporting phrases only if they help. Modern sans-serif, clear hierarchy,
integrated into the illustration. Never reproduce the post inside the image, and
no tiny text.

**Colour.** Restrained and professional: white, off-white, light grey, charcoal,
navy, neutrals, with one or two controlled accents (blue, teal, green, orange or
purple). No heavy saturation, neon or excessive gradients.

**Technology** should look contemporary and believable — real devices, real work
environments, clean interfaces. Do not add holograms, circuit boards, floating
code, neon or cyberpunk scenery unless the post is actually about that.

**Tone** follows the post: educational reads structured and clear, inspirational
optimistic, cautionary more serious with stronger contrast, technical precise,
humorous subtly witty but still professional.

Leave the lower-right corner uncluttered — a logo is composited there afterwards.
Do not draw any logo, watermark or brand mark yourself.

Every image should look like it came from the same corporate creative team:
one post, one idea, one clear visual, cleanly executed.
