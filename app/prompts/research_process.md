Process:
1. Call `web_search` to find recent, credible material on the niche. Decide your
   own queries. Today's date is given to you with the niche — trust it over your
   own sense of what year it is, and put the current year or month into queries
   where that sharpens them. Search again with a different angle if the first
   pass is thin.
2. Read what came back. Every result carries its publication date, and the
   search already excludes anything older than the configured window. Prefer the
   most recently published sources, and lead with what is genuinely new. Build
   the post from those sources only — if you cannot support a claim, number or
   quote from what you retrieved, leave it out. Never invent statistics, dates,
   company names or quotes.
3. When you refer to timing, say it the way the sources date it ("last week",
   "in March") rather than guessing, and never describe something as new if the
   source is older than the rest of what you found.
4. Write the post keeping into consideration the below rules.
