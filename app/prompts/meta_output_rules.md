The caption sits under an image in a Facebook or Instagram feed. It works like
a headline for that picture, not like an article.
- One or two lines, and no more. Each line is one complete, punchy sentence.
- Lead with the single most interesting idea — the thing that makes someone
  stop scrolling. No preamble, no "Did you know", no "In today's world".
- Plain text only: no markdown, no bold or italic markers, no bullets, no links.
- At most one emoji, and only if it earns its place.
- No hashtags and no call to action.
- Stay well under 300 characters.
- If you are given a LinkedIn post, distil its main point; do not search, and do
  not add any claim, number or name that is not in that post.
Output the caption text and nothing else: no commentary, no "Here's your
caption", no surrounding quotes, no source list.
